Clean up your phone's media

Connect your Android phone, scan WhatsApp media, and decide what to keep or delete.

1 Enable USB Debugging on your phone (Settings → Developer Options)
2 Connect via USB and tap Allow on the phone prompt
3 Click Scan Media to browse your WhatsApp files
100% local & private. Everything runs on your machine. Files transfer directly via ADB over USB. No AI, no cloud, no data leaves your computer.
